📏 1. Specimen Dimensions & Math Verification ASTM D1002 Standard

To shear the adhesive line cleanly before SUPERWOOD suffers tensile failure, adhesive force ($\mathbf{F_{\text{glue}}}$) must induce a peak stress ($\sigma_{\text{wood}}$) below SUPERWOOD's 150 MPa Modulus of Rupture (MOR).

$$\sigma_{\text{wood}} = \frac{4 \cdot F_{\text{glue}}}{w \cdot t} = \frac{4 \cdot \tau_{\text{glue}} \cdot L}{t}$$
Engineered Dimensions: Thickness $t = 6.0\text{ mm}$, Width $w = 20.0\text{ mm}$, Overlap $L = 5.0\text{ mm}$, Piece Length = $100\text{ mm}$ (Assembly $195\text{ mm}$).
Math Verification Check:
• Glue Shear Load: $15\text{ MPa} \times (20\text{ mm} \times 5\text{ mm}) = \mathbf{1,500\text{ N (1.5 kN)}}$.
• Peak SUPERWOOD Stress: $\frac{4 \times 1500\text{ N}}{20\text{ mm} \times 6\text{ mm}} = \mathbf{50.0\text{ MPa}}$ *(Safely below 150 MPa MOR)*.
⚙️ 2. Instron Grip Setup & Alignment Shims

Offset coupon geometries cause diagonal twisting under load. Insert 6 mm thick shims in upper and lower grips using scrap SUPERWOOD to maintain axial alignment.

01

02

03

04

05

🧪 3. Test Execution & Failure Modes

Pull at a quasi-static rate of 1.30 mm/min ($0.05\text{ in/min}$). Compute shear strength $\tau_{\text{app}} = \frac{F_{\text{peak}}}{w \cdot L}$.

Post-Test Failure Diagnostics:
Cohesive Wood Substrate tears out; bond exceeds wood strength.
Adhesive (Interfacial) Clean detachment at interface; prep flaw.
Cohesive Adhesive Polymer tears down center; true shear strength.
📐 4. Mechanics: Width Cancellation Theorem

Specimen width ($w$) cancels out mathematically from substrate normal stress:

$$\sigma_{\text{wood}} = \frac{4 \cdot (\tau_{\text{glue}} \cdot w \cdot L)}{w \cdot t} \implies \sigma_{\text{wood}} = \frac{4 \cdot \tau_{\text{glue}} \cdot L}{t}$$

Key Insight: Substrate stress depends solely on the ratio of Overlap to Thickness ($\frac{L}{t}$). 20 mm width was chosen to eliminate edge defect artifacts and operate at $1.5\text{ kN}$ load cell sweet spot.
